03.Vocabulary: top parts

The most-used LEGO parts. Click any cell for details.

What is a treemap?

A treemap divides a rectangle into tiles sized proportionally to a value. Each tile is a part; the area = usage count.

4-4cyli.dat (Technic cylinder) occupies ~36% of the visible area: 20,031 uses, the most-used primitive in the corpus.

Hover for part names. Click a tile to highlight it across the dashboard.

How to read the treemap

Parts are sorted by frequency. The first 4-5 parts cover the bulk. The remaining 20 fill the gaps.

4-4cyli.dat is the Technic cylinder primitive used for pin holes, axles, and pivots. Its dominance reflects how Technic sets (175 in the corpus) use thousands of these.

04.Y-layers (vertical rhythm)

How high pieces sit in models. Multiples of 8 = plate, 24 = brick.

What are Y-layers?

The Y-axis in LDraw is vertical. Y=0 is the ground; Y=-24 is one brick up. Most pieces sit at multiples of 8 LDU (plate height).

The bar chart shows the 15 most common Y values across all 534K pieces. Notice the spike at Y=0 (base plane) and the alternation -8 / -24 (plate, brick, plate, brick...).

05.Chaining: how pieces fit together

Deltas (Δx, Δy, Δz) measure the offset between consecutive placed pieces.

What are deltas?

When an LDraw file says place piece A at (0,0,0), then piece B at (20, -8, 0), the delta is (20, -8, 0) — meaning "20 LDU in +X, 8 LDU down".

Multiples of 20 LDU in X/Z = perfect stud alignment. Multiples of 8 LDU in Y = plate height.

What are bigrams?

A bigram is a 2-piece sequence: which part B tends to follow which part A. They reveal assembly patterns.

E.g., 3004 -> 3004 = "another brick 1x2 follows the previous brick 1x2" (90% of bigrams are self-bigrams, meaning rows of the same part).

07.Reflections & BFC: the Space surprise

The biggest revelation: Space-themed sets use reflections heavily.

What is a scatter plot?

Each dot is one theme. X axis = % of sets with BFC CERTIFY. Y axis = % of pieces with reflections (matrices with det < 0).

The red dots in the top-right are Space-themed sets — they break the convention more than anywhere else.

Classic Town (109 sets) sits in the lower-left: low reflections, modest BFC.

Top themes by reflections count

Classic Space has 196 reflections despite only 8 sets. Unitron has 347 (4 sets, 0.55% of corpus).

These numbers explain why the global ratio of 1.345% reflections looks high: 87.8% come from Space-themed sets.

09.Custom parts (embedded)

Sets that include their own parts (not in the official library).

What are custom parts?

An embedded custom part is a .dat file inside an MPD whose name starts with the set number (e.g. 10252 - 24599s01.dat). It exists only for that set.

Fabuland sets often have many custom parts because the Fabuland theme has unique piece designs.

11.Methodology & limitations

Pipeline

  1. Scraped OMR catalog (1,470 sets).
  2. Sampled 8 cohorts covering 1,438 sets.
  3. Downloaded each MPD via library.ldraw.org/library/omr/{SET}-{Q}.mpd.
  4. Parsed all MPDs with analysis/batch_parse3.py.
  5. Aggregated per cohort with the same script.
  6. Dispatched 4 parallel subagents for analysis.
  7. Derived 23 conventions (7+8+2+6 by status).

Limitations

  • OMR has 1,470 sets total; we sampled 1,438 (97.8%).
  • OMR is curated, not exhaustive — many modern sets are missing.
  • Pirates theme is dominated by 6286 (86% of the theme).
  • Textures (TEXMAP) not analyzed.
  • No comparison with MOCs.
